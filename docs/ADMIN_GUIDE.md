# Owner's guide to the Ayudhas admin

Open **https://gunwatts.github.io/Homemadefood-Ayudhas-Frontend/admin/** (or `/admin/` on your custom domain) on your phone or computer and sign in with your email and
password. The admin works well on a phone.

> **Remember:** a request on the website is **not** an order. Nothing has been paid or
> booked. You decide, on WhatsApp, whether you can fulfil it, what the delivery charge is,
> and how payment happens.

---

## Every business day (5 minutes)

1. Open **Dashboard**. Red tiles mean new requests are waiting.
2. Open **Order requests**. For each *New* request:
   - Tap **WhatsApp** — a message opens with the reference, items and subtotal already
     typed. Add what you need to say and press Send.
   - Confirm: is everything available? Which receiving option (Delhivery, pickup, friend)?
   - For **Delhivery**, tap *Check serviceability & rates on Delhivery*, check the PIN code
     and parcel weight in your Delhivery account, and tell the customer the courier charge.
   - Agree payment directly with the customer. Ask for the full address on WhatsApp only
     if you are going ahead.
   - Change the **Status**: *Contacted* → *Confirmed by vendor* → *Completed*
     (or *Declined*, or *Spam*).
   - Use **Private notes** for things like "courier ₹90 quoted, paid by UPI". Customers never
     see notes.
3. Open **Callback requests** and call or WhatsApp those customers the same way.
4. The site tells customers when to expect a reply (*Site text & settings → Business →
   Expected response time*). Keep that promise, or change the wording.

If email alerts are set up, you also get an email for each new request. If an email fails,
the request is still in the inbox.

## Products

**Catalogue → Products**

- **Hide a product** (out of stock, out of season): switch **Visible** off. It disappears
  from the website at once. Past requests keep the product name. Prefer hiding over
  deleting.
- **Change a price**: *Edit* → *Pack sizes & product-only prices*. Enter the price of the
  product only. **Never include delivery** in the price.
- **Add a pack size**: *+ Add pack size* (e.g. "1 kg", "20 pieces"). Untick *Show* to hide
  one pack size only.
- **Add a product**: *+ Add product*. Fill the name, category, short description, at least
  one pack size with a price, and ideally a photo. New products start **hidden** — switch on
  *Visible on website* when the details are correct.
- **Featured** products appear on the home page.

### What to write (important)
- List ingredients exactly as you make the product, and always list allergens
  (mustard, sesame, groundnut/peanut, milk/ghee, wheat, nuts…).
- Give honest storage and shelf-life guidance.
- **Do not** write health claims ("good for diabetes", "boosts immunity"), and do not use
  words like *organic*, *chemical-free* or *preservative-free*, or mention certifications,
  unless you have proof.

## Photos

- Use **real photos** of your products, kitchen and village — not pictures from the internet.
- Take photos in daylight, landscape (sideways) if possible. Phone photos are fine: the
  website shrinks and compresses them automatically and removes location data.
- Add a short description of each photo (helps blind visitors and Google).
- **Gallery**: *+ Upload photos* lets you choose many at once. Pick a *section*
  (Products, Preparation, Village, The maker).
- Storage is limited (shown on the Dashboard). Delete photos you no longer use.

## Videos

Upload the video to your YouTube channel first (public or unlisted), then in **Videos**
paste the YouTube link. You can attach a video to a product so it shows on that product's
page, and tick *Show on home page* for one video.

## Banners

Short promotions on the home page, e.g. "Diwali orders close on 25 October". Up to two are
shown. Switch *Visible* off when the promotion ends.

## Site text & settings

- **Business**: your WhatsApp number (with country code, digits only, e.g.
  `919876543210`), phone, location, delivery note, response time, FSSAI number, and the
  private email for alerts.
- **Home page / Our story**: your words and photos. Write in your own voice.
- **Legal**: privacy notice and terms. If you make a significant change to the privacy
  notice, also change *Privacy notice version* (e.g. to today's date).
- **Site switches**:
  - *Accept order requests on the website* — switch off during holidays or if you get spam.
    WhatsApp buttons keep working.
  - *Show 'sample content' notice* — switch off only when every product, price and text
    has been checked.

Changes appear on the website the next time a visitor opens a page.

## Your account and customer privacy

**Account & data**

- Use a long password (12+ characters) that you don't use anywhere else.
- Turn on **Two-step sign-in**: scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or Microsoft
  Authenticator. You'll then type a 6-digit code when signing in.
- Lost your phone or forgot your password? Ask your developer — they can reset it.
- **Customer numbers are private.** Use them only to reply about the request. Don't add
  customers to broadcast lists without their permission.
- If a customer asks you to delete their details: search their number in the inbox and
  press **Delete** on each of their requests.
- Once a month, press **Delete expired finished requests** (removes completed, declined,
  closed and spam requests older than about 6 months).
- **Export CSV** downloads requests for your own records. Keep that file private and delete
  it when you no longer need it.
